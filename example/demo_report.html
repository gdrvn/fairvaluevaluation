Methodology

US-listed equities, market cap above $10 billion, USD price and financial reporting, estimated fair value 15–60% above market price, at least two surviving valuation models. Six models are attempted when suitable: simplified 5-year equity free-cash-flow DCF (10.5% discount and 2.5% terminal growth), forward P/E peer median, EV/EBITDA peer median, Graham number, residual income for lenders, and shareholder yield. Reverse DCF is informational only. Outlier rules: remove values outside 0–3× price and below 0.4× or above 3× other-model median; weighted average of survivors. Sector peers are built from the fetched sample; no fabricated peer estimates.

Fair value is not a time-specific price target. Yahoo data may be delayed, incomplete or erroneous. Financial figures are cached for up to the configured hours, so this is not a genuine tick-by-tick repricing service. Growth and discount-rate assumptions are simplified; this does not reproduce ACCE Investments' implementation. Large disagreement between models signals low confidence.
